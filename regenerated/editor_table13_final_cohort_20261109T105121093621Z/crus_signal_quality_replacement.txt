The final primary test cohort comprised 257 Cascade forced predictions, including 181 correct and 76 incorrect predictions. Mean CRUS was 0.103103 for correct predictions and 0.227043 for incorrect predictions. The mean difference (incorrect minus correct) was 0.123940, with a conditional 95% group-bootstrap percentile interval of [0.100999, 0.146230] based on 10,000 valid replicates. An exploratory two-sided Mann–Whitney comparison, with incorrect cases as the first sample, gave U=11371.000000, p=1.44684e-16, and signed rank-biserial effect r=0.653242. The record-level p-value is not cluster-adjusted. Observed mean CRUS was higher among incorrect predictions. This analysis characterizes association with prediction correctness within the fixed primary evaluation; it does not establish optimal weights, independent external validity, or clinical harm reduction.
